The Rule of 72. Double your money in your head. Want to know how long an investment takes to double? Divide 72 by the annual return. At 7 percent, about ten years. At 9 percent, about eight. It is a shortcut for exponential growth accurate enough for mental math — and a quick reality check on any “too good” return. The takeaway.
