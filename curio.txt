Why EV range drops in the cold. Your BC winter costs you kilometres. Cold slows the chemical reactions inside the battery, so it delivers less energy — and heating the cabin draws power straight from the pack instead of from waste engine heat. Together these can cut range 20 to 40 percent on a frosty morning. Preconditioning while plugged in warms the battery using grid power, not your range. The takeaway.
