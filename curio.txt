What an AI is really doing. It is an extremely good next-word guesser. A large language model turns your words into numbers, then predicts the most likely next chunk of text, over and over, one piece at a time. It has no database it looks things up in — the “knowledge” is baked into billions of tuned weights. Fluent reasoning emerges from prediction at massive scale. The takeaway.
