Antibiotic resistance is evolution, sped up. Pharmacy meets natural selection. Every antibiotic course is a selection pressure: the susceptible bacteria die, and any rare survivor with a resistance trait now has the field to itself and multiplies. Because bacteria reproduce in minutes and swap resistance genes like trading cards, this is evolution you can watch in real time — and why using antibiotics only when needed protects the whole toolkit.
