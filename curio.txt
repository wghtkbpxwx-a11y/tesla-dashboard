Why time zones exist. Blame the railways. For most of history every town set noon by its own Sun, so clocks differed town to town. That was fine until trains made schedules impossible across dozens of local times. Railways pushed for standardized zones in the 1800s, and the world was carved into hourly slices. Your clock is really a shared agreement, not a fact of nature. The takeaway. The time on your wall is a human convention, invented to make trains run on time. Here is a question.
