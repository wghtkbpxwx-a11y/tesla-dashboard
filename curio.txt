Why meds take days to “kick in”. The magic number is about five. Start a regular dose and the drug builds up until the amount you take each interval equals the amount your body clears. That balance point is called steady state. It takes roughly four to five half-lives to get there — no matter the dose. A drug with a 24-hour half-life needs about five days before it is doing its full, steady job. The takeaway. Feeling “nothing yet” on day two can be normal pharmacokinetics, not a failed drug.
