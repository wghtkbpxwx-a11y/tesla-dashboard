Why you can’t unscramble an egg. The one law time obeys. The second law of thermodynamics says disorder — entropy — tends to increase. There are countless messy arrangements of a broken egg and only a few tidy ones, so nature drifts toward mess. That statistical one-way street is why heat flows hot to cold, and arguably why time seems to run forward at all. The takeaway. The arrow of time is really just probability — order is rare, disorder is easy. Here is a question. What does the second law of thermodynamics say tends to happen? A. Energy disappears. B.
