One ticker, thousands of companies. What you actually own when you buy X E Q T. An all-equity index fund like X E Q T holds a slice of thousands of companies across Canada, the US, and the world — automatically weighted and rebalanced. Instead of betting on a single winner, you own the whole market’s growth. Diversification is the rare “free lunch”: it lowers risk without necessarily lowering expected return. The takeaway.
