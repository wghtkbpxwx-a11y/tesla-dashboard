A drug drip is a thermostat. Pharmacy meets control engineering. Titrating a medication — nudging the dose up or down toward a target effect — is exactly what a thermostat does with temperature: measure, compare to a setpoint, adjust, repeat. This feedback loop shows up everywhere: insulin pumps, cruise control, even how your body holds its own temperature. Same idea, different setpoint. The takeaway.
