Bigger than Central Park. A hometown fact for the drive. Vancouver’s Stanley Park is about 400 hectares — roughly 20 percent larger than New York’s Central Park, and it is ringed by a seawall you can walk or bike almost 9 kilometres around. Most of it is near-natural rainforest, with some trees centuries old, sitting minutes from downtown. The takeaway.
