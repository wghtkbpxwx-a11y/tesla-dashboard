Why the Moon shows one face. We have never seen its far side from Earth. The Moon spins on its axis in exactly the time it takes to orbit Earth — so the same side always faces us. This “tidal locking” came from Earth’s gravity gently braking the Moon’s spin over eons. The hidden far side is not dark — it gets just as much sun. It is simply never pointed our way. The takeaway.
