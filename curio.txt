Regenerative braking. Your motor moonlights as a generator. When you lift off the accelerator, the electric motor runs backwards as a generator, turning the car’s motion back into electricity and feeding it to the battery. That is why EVs use “one-pedal driving” and why their brake pads can last far longer than a gas car’s. The takeaway.
